# MINDO Snacks

**Snack Smart. Live Sharp.**

MINDO is a proposed Egyptian functional-snack brand targeting young people aged 18–30. The brand focuses on convenient snacking for busy lifestyles, including studying, working, fitness, and everyday activities.

### Project Focus
- Market and competitor analysis
- Target audience and buyer persona
- Brand positioning and differentiation
- Social media strategy and content planning
- Campaign concepts and creative content
- Marketing objectives and KPIs

**Campaign Concept:** Fuel Your Next Move.

**Project Type:** Digital Marketing & Brand Strategy
